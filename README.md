### 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **CartoFriche**, a platform developed by CEREMA that inventories brownfield sites across France.

The platform centralizes territorial, land and environmental data to support local authorities and urban development stakeholders in brownfield rehabilitation projects.

The main challenge identified was not the lack of available data, but the difficulty for users to **access, interpret and use this information efficiently to support decision-making**.

Our objective was therefore to design a data-driven solution capable of improving access to information, helping users prioritize brownfield sites and supporting decision-making.

---

### 🎯 Business Challenges

The Discovery phase highlighted three main user challenges:

- Difficulty prioritizing brownfield sites according to specific project needs
- Complex data and interfaces for non-expert users
- Lack of transparency and collaboration in rehabilitation project monitoring

These findings led us to focus the project on three areas:

**Decision Support → Data Accessibility → Collaboration**

---

### 🔎 Discovery & User Research

The Discovery phase was used to understand the ecosystem, stakeholders and user needs before designing the solution.

The approach included:

`Context Analysis → Mission Model Canvas → Stakeholder Analysis → Personas → Experience Maps → Problem Definition → Benchmark`

Three user profiles were studied:

- Urban Planning Project Manager
- Land Development Manager
- Environmental Association Coordinator

Despite different objectives, they shared similar difficulties: multiple data sources, heterogeneous information, time-consuming verification and difficulty turning available data into actionable decisions.

---

### 🧩 From User Needs to Product Priorities

The analysis showed that the main issue was not data availability, but **data exploitation**.

A prioritization framework was used to evaluate potential solutions according to their impact on:

- User satisfaction
- Business value
- Internal efficiency
- Growth
- Implementation effort

The two main priorities identified were:

**1. Develop decision intelligence**

**2. Simplify data exploitation**

---

### 📊 KPI Framework

KPIs were defined to evaluate both solution adoption and its impact on decision-making.

Examples include:

- Average time required to identify a relevant brownfield site
- Number of qualified brownfield sites
- Usage rate of decision-support features
- User satisfaction
- Adoption of scoring and recommendation tools

The objective is to measure whether the solution effectively reduces research time and improves the decision-making process.

---

### 💡 Proposed Solution — SmartFriche

The proposed solution is a **Business Intelligence MVP** called **SmartFriche**.

Its objective is to provide a consolidated and intuitive view of brownfield data while supporting users throughout the decision-making process.

The dashboard is structured around three main dimensions:

#### 🎯 Brownfield Prioritization
Identify and compare sites according to their potential.

#### 🗺️ Access & Visualization
Provide simple access to territorial information through interactive visualization.

#### 🤝 Monitoring & Collaboration
Improve visibility and information sharing throughout rehabilitation projects.

---

### 🚀 MVP Features

The Minimum Viable Product includes:

- Interactive brownfield mapping
- Search and filtering engine
- Visualization of key site characteristics
- Decision-support indicators
- Simplified scoring system

The MVP intentionally focuses on high-value and understandable features rather than advanced AI or complex predictive models.

---

### ⚠️ Risks & Limitations

**Data quality and availability**

Data comes from multiple sources and may be incomplete, outdated or dependent on manual updates.

**Decision-making risk**

The scoring system is designed to support decision-making, not replace human expertise. Local constraints and field realities cannot always be captured by a simplified score.

**Regulatory and security constraints**

Data usage, distribution and storage must take regulatory and security requirements into account.

A real-world implementation would also require validation with actual users to confirm and refine the assumptions made during the Discovery phase.
